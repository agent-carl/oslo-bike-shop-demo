What we can help with

From a flat tyre to a full service. We explain what needs doing and never start work you have not approved.

Service

We check gears, brakes, chain and wheels, then adjust and lubricate. Your bike will feel like new.

Repairs

Flat tyres, a skipping chain, weak brakes, broken spokes and wobbly wheels.

E-bikes

Diagnostics, software updates and service for e-bikes from the most common brands.

Winter ready

Studded tyres, mudguards and lights, so you get around safely when the streets are icy.

Questions and answers

Do I need to book a time?

For a service or bigger repairs, yes. We can often fix a flat tyre the same day without booking.

How long does a service take?

Usually 1–3 days, depending on the season and whether we need to order parts.

Do you repair e-bikes?

Yes. We diagnose motor, battery and display issues, and service the rest of the bike.

What if the repair costs more than agreed?

We contact you before doing anything else. You never pay for work you have not approved.
